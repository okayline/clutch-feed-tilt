# CLUTCH FEED TILT Changelog

Newest first. Times are Hawaii time (HST).

---

## v0.2 — "Log In"
**Wed Oct 21, 2026 · 7:25 AM HST**

- **ACCOUNT, under the screen.** Make a save code or add a passkey, and log in as your player on any browser or device. They're the same save codes and passkeys as the other 88x31 games. Logging out needs a save code or passkey first, so you can't lock yourself out. Forgetting a code, removing a passkey or logging out asks for a second tap.
- **You have a name.** Your player gets a random two-word name, shown in the squad panel instead of YOU once you have a player.
- Removed: the game working with no database. It now has its own (cft-db) for players and devices; runs still aren't saved.

## v0.1 — "1v3"
**Wed Oct 21, 2026 · 7:20 AM HST**

- **First version.** Today's corridor, the same for everyone, drawn in ASCII inside a 4Dungeon-style frame. Your squad of 4 pushes in and falls, then it's you against 3. Tap the enemies before they fire. Miss in a row and your crosshair shakes. At the end the run calls you CLUTCH, FEED or TILT.
- Runs aren't saved yet, and the teammates are stand-in names, not other players' runs. Both come with saved runs and the ledger.
- Removed: nothing (first version).
