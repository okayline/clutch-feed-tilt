## v0.2 — "Log In"
**Wed Oct 21, 2026 · 7:25 AM HST**

- **[U]SER, in the bottom bar** (or press U). It opens in the 3D view like 4Dungeon's USER panel, and the run waits. Make a save code (copy it or download it as a text file) or add a passkey, and log in as your player on any browser or device. They're the same save codes and passkeys as the other 88x31 games. Logging out shows only once you have a save code or passkey, so you can't lock yourself out.
- **No text glow.** Bright and gold text is flat now.
- **The game sits in the middle of the window,** across and down, instead of hugging the top. Removed: the fixed gap at the top of the page.
- **You have a name.** Your player gets a random two-word name, shown in the squad panel instead of YOU once you have a player.
- Removed: the glow on bright and gold text, and the game working with no database. It now has its own (cft-db) for players and devices; runs still aren't saved.

## v0.1 — "1v3"
**Wed Oct 21, 2026 · 7:20 AM HST**

- **First version.** Today's corridor, the same for everyone, drawn in ASCII inside a 4Dungeon-style frame. Your squad of 4 pushes in and falls, then it's you against 3. Tap the enemies before they fire. Miss in a row and your crosshair shakes. At the end the run calls you CLUTCH, FEED or TILT.
- Runs aren't saved yet, and the teammates are stand-in names, not other players' runs. Both come with saved runs and the ledger.
- Removed: nothing (first version).
